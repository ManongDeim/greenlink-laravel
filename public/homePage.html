Events

Imagine a serene evening where the sky transforms into a canvas of glowing dreams. The Sky Lantern Parade is a magical event where participants gather to release lanterns, each carrying hopes, dreams, and wishes.

Participants light their lanterns, and with a gentle release, the night sky comes alive with floating lights, creating a scene straight out of a fairytale. The lanterns, made from eco-friendly materials, drift upwards, illuminating the darkness with their warm glow. This enchanting spectacle symbolizes personal aspirations and fosters a sense of community.

The parade is accompanied by live music, captivating performances, and delicious local food, making it a perfect blend of cultural celebration and personal reflection. Whether you’re seeking a romantic evening, a family adventure, or a moment of peace, the Sky Lantern Parade offers an unforgettable experience.

About Us

Lola Sayong Eco Surf-Farm is a community-driven space in Sorsogon where nature, culture, and people come together. We offer eco-friendly venues for celebrations, farm-to-table dining, event booking, and room reservations — giving you a relaxing escape by the sea.
